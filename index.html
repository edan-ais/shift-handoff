Shift Handoff

Turn end-of-shift notes into a clear handoff for the next shift. Runs entirely in your browser. No account, no server.

1Paste your shift notes

One line per item. Start a line with ! or incident: to mark an incident.

2Review and assign

Add an owner and due date to each item. Anything missing one is highlighted, not blocked.
